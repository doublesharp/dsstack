# Branding and sharing details

Include relevant finishing details in the surface being evaluated; their acceptance depends on the requested product and audience.

- **Identity:** coherent logo/wordmark, favicon, touch/app icons, manifest icons and theme colors where used. Check real asset loading, sizing, transparent backgrounds, and light/dark variants.
- **Iconography:** consider a consistent custom set when stock icons weaken clarity or identity. Reuse the project's SVG/icon pipeline; check actual control sizes, labels, accessible names, and familiar meanings.
- **Page and sharing metadata:** meaningful titles/descriptions, Open Graph and social-card tags, preview image, canonical URL, and share links for public pages. Check that absolute URLs and images resolve for the intended environment and page, rather than merely finding tags in source.
- **Final polish:** readable typography and fallback fonts, useful empty/error/loading states, focus indicators, correct links, and consistent asset treatment. Score observed user-facing quality; a new asset or tag alone earns no design gain.

Keep metadata and branding changes within the agreed surface. Missing public-page metadata is relevant to sharing quality, not an excuse for an unrelated sitewide rewrite.
